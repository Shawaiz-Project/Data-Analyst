Key Findings

insights
  • Largest drop‑off occurs between Month 0 and Month 1 — retention falls from 100% to ~35%.
  • Retention continues to decline steadily; Month 12 retention is typically below 20%.
  • Recent cohorts (post‑Jul 2011) show slightly weaker retention than historical cohorts, especially in early months.
  • Revenue retention declines more slowly than customer retention, indicating returning customers spend more.
  • The gap between customer and revenue retention widens with cohort age, suggesting a shift in purchase behaviour among retained customers.
