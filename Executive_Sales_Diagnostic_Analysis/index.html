Methodology & KPI Definitions

How the numbers are produced, validated and reconciled. Nothing here is decorative.

Dataset

Classification: CANCELLATION → InvoiceNo prefix “C” · ADJUSTMENT → negative price or service code with ≤ 0 qty · INVALID → zero qty or zero price · SALE → everything else. Exact duplicates removed (logged below); no silent row deletion. Missing CustomerID rows stay in revenue but are excluded from every retention metric.
